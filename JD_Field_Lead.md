## Role Overview

As a Field Lead at 3 T's Engineering.AI, you'll manage, lead, and physically work along concrete crews on active job sites, overseeing all aspects of concrete resurfacing and exterior work. This is a true leadership position—you'll coordinate crews, ensure quality execution, drive safety culture, and serve as the main point of contact between your team and project management. You'll have the autonomy to make decisions that impact your crew and your job site, and the opportunity to develop your skills as a leader while staying connected to the craft you know.

This is the bridge role in our proven advancement pathway: **Installer → Field Lead → Superintendent**. Field Leads who demonstrate strong management, safety, and business acumen move into superintendent roles, where profit sharing kicks in and you share directly in 3 T's Engineering.AI's success.

## Key Responsibilities

### Crew Leadership & Management
- Lead, supervise, and mentor a field crew (typically 2–4 people) on assigned projects
- Assign daily tasks and coordinate crew workflow to meet project deadlines and specifications
- Ensure all crew members understand their responsibilities and project objectives
- Identify and develop crew members for advancement; provide mentoring and coaching
- Foster a collaborative, professional work environment where your team feels valued and supported

### Quality & Execution
- Oversee all concrete resurfacing, finishing, and exterior work to ensure it meets or exceeds company standards and client expectations
- Inspect work in progress and completed sections; catch issues early and correct them immediately
- Read and interpret job specifications and work orders
- Make real-time decisions to adjust approach and solve field problems as they arise
- Maintain consistency in quality across all aspects of each project

### Safety & Compliance
- Lead by example: follow all safety protocols and OSHA guidelines without exception
- Conduct pre-shift safety meetings and toolbox talks with your crew
- Perform daily site safety inspections and identify and address hazards immediately
- Enforce PPE requirements and safe equipment use at all times
- Report any incidents, near-misses, or safety concerns to management promptly
- Ensure compliance with all applicable building codes and permit requirements

### Equipment, Tools & Materials
- Monitor and maintain all equipment and tools assigned to your crew
- Coordinate material orders and deliveries to keep the job on schedule
- Manage inventory; prevent waste and ensure efficient use of company resources
- Report equipment issues or maintenance needs immediately

### Communication & Coordination
- Communicate regularly with customers and leadership on progress, timeline, budget, and any issues
- Coordinate with other trades and stakeholders on shared job sites
- Provide crew members with clear direction and timely feedback
- Document daily work activities, crew hours, and materials used
- Keep meticulous records and submit reports on time

### Growth & Development
- Actively seek feedback and suggestions from your team and management
- Participate in training opportunities to improve your leadership, technical, and business skills
- Learn business fundamentals that prepare you for superintendent responsibilities
- Demonstrate initiative in identifying process improvements and cost efficiencies

## Career Growth

The 3 T's Engineering.AI advancement pathway is clear and achievable:

**Installer (0–3 years):** Learn the craft, master concrete resurfacing, build safety and reliability foundation.

**Field Lead (3–5+ years):** Step into leadership while staying hands-on. Manage crews, drive quality and safety, learn business fundamentals. *You are here.*

**Superintendent (5+ years):** Lead multiple crews and projects. Manage P&L, make strategic decisions, participate in profit sharing through our Profit First model and open-book financial system.

When you reach superintendent level, you're not just managing projects—you're a partner in the company's success. You'll see the numbers, understand how decisions impact profitability, and share directly in the bottom line.
