Field Lead — Concrete Resurfacing & Exterior Services

Leadership role managing crews and jobs. Earned by proven Installers who show leadership potential. Clear path to Superintendent with profit sharing and open-book financials.

Chesterfield, MO Full-Time, 1099 Superintendent Track
Apply Now

Role Overview

As a Field Lead at 3T's Engineering, you'll manage, lead, and physically work alongside concrete crews on active job sites, overseeing all aspects of concrete resurfacing and exterior work. You'll coordinate crews, ensure quality execution, drive safety culture, and serve as the main point of contact between your team and project management.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead, supervise, and mentor a field crew (typically 2-4 people) on assigned projects
  • Assign daily tasks and coordinate crew workflow to meet project deadlines
  • Oversee all concrete resurfacing, finishing, and exterior work to company standards
  • Lead by example: follow all safety protocols and OSHA guidelines without exception
  • Conduct pre-shift safety meetings and daily site safety inspections
  • Monitor and maintain all equipment and tools assigned to your crew
  • Communicate regularly with customers and leadership on progress and issues
  • Document daily work activities, crew hours, and materials used

What You Bring

  • 3-5 years hands-on experience in concrete work, construction, or related trades
  • Proven experience in a leadership or supervisory role (crew lead, foreman, etc.)
  • Strong working knowledge of concrete resurfacing, finishing, and exterior work
  • Problem-solving mindset — you troubleshoot issues quickly under pressure
  • Valid driver's license with reliable transportation
  • Ability to pass a background check and drug screening

Bonus Points

  • Concrete, forming, or hardscape experience
  • Previous concrete foreman or equivalent trade experience
  • Customer service or client-facing experience
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish)

What We Provide

  • Complete tool & equipment package — no personal investment required
  • Full PPE: hard hats, safety glasses, gloves, work boots, and all required gear
  • Competitive pay with quarterly performance bonuses
  • On-the-job mentorship and leadership development
  • OSHA certification, first aid/CPR, and ongoing safety training
  • Business acumen education — learn P&L management as you prepare for Superintendent
  • Year-round work with consistent projects

Career Path

Installer (0-3 years): Learn the craft, master concrete resurfacing, build safety and reliability foundation.

Field Lead (3-5+ years): Step into leadership while staying hands-on. Manage crews, drive quality and safety. You are here.

Superintendent (5+ years): Lead multiple crews and projects. Manage P&L, make strategic decisions, participate in profit sharing through our open-book financial system.

Decorative Concrete Installer

The entry point to your career with 3T's. Setting forms, pouring, finishing, and breaking down. Hard physical work with real pay, training, and a clear path to Field Lead and beyond for those who prove themselves.

Chesterfield, MO Full-Time, 1099 Path to Field Lead
Apply Now

Role Overview

As a Decorative Concrete Installer, you'll be at the heart of our operations — learning, building, and mastering the craft of decorative concrete work. You'll work alongside experienced installers and field leads on residential and commercial projects throughout the St. Louis area. No prior concrete experience required — we'll train you.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare concrete surfaces for resurfacing: grinding, etching, pressure washing, and removing debris
  • Mix and apply concrete overlays, epoxies, sealers, and specialty coatings
  • Operate hand tools and power equipment: trowels, grinders, polishers, mixers
  • Apply decorative finishes including stamped patterns, color stains, and sealants
  • Screed, float, and finish concrete surfaces to smooth, uniform results
  • Work on hands and knees, lift and carry materials (up to 100 lbs regularly)
  • Maintain clean, organized job sites and properly store all tools and equipment
  • Follow all safety protocols, wear required PPE, and work safely in all weather

What You Bring

  • Physically fit — can regularly lift 50-100 lbs, work on hands/knees, and perform demanding labor
  • Reliable — show up ready to work, on time, committed to the job
  • Willing to learn — open to training and mastering new skills
  • Team player — communicate well, follow instructions, work collaboratively
  • Valid driver's license with reliable transportation
  • Able to pass a drug test and background check

Bonus Points

  • Any concrete forming experience (huge plus)
  • Flatwork, hardscape, or paving background
  • Familiarity with hand tools, power tools, and construction equipment
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish)

What We Provide

  • All hand tools, power equipment, and specialty tools provided
  • All PPE: gloves, safety glasses, respirators, harnesses as needed
  • Competitive pay commensurate with experience and performance
  • On-the-job mentorship from experienced installers and field leads
  • Comprehensive training in concrete finishing, stamping, sealing, and resurfacing
  • Overtime available and regular, steady work

Career Path

Decorative Concrete Installer (your starting role): Learn the craft, build your skills, prove your reliability.

Field Lead (12-24 months): Lead smaller crews, manage project details, earn supervisor compensation.

Superintendent: Manage multiple crews/projects, oversee quality, profit sharing and open-book financials.
