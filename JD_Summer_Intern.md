## About 3T's Engineering.AI

3T's Engineering.AI is a growing blue-collar company specializing in high-quality concrete resurfacing, decorative concrete finishes, soft washing, power washing, and exterior solutions across the St. Louis metropolitan area. We pride ourselves on craftsmanship, reliability, and building a team that genuinely enjoys what they do. We use cutting-edge AI to automate where possible while partnering with our employees to ensure a high-quality product.

We're offering a hands-on summer internship for students and early-career candidates who want real field experience in the skilled trades.

---

## Role Overview

This paid summer internship gives you hands-on experience in decorative concrete installation, exterior services, and field operations. You'll work alongside experienced installers and field leads on real job sites — residential and commercial projects throughout the St. Louis metro area.

This is physical, outdoor work. You'll learn by doing: preparing surfaces, mixing materials, operating tools, and seeing projects from start to finish. If you've ever been curious about the construction trades, this is your chance to try it with real pay, real mentorship, and a real path to a full-time career.

**No prior experience required.** We'll train you on everything.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Daily Job Site Work
- Assist with concrete surface preparation: grinding, etching, pressure washing, and debris removal
- Help mix and apply concrete overlays, epoxies, sealers, and specialty coatings
- Learn to operate hand tools and power equipment under supervision (trowels, grinders, polishers, mixers)
- Support decorative finishing work: stamped patterns, color stains, protective sealants
- Assist with material handling, loading/unloading, and job site setup/breakdown
- Work on hands and knees, lift and carry materials (up to 80 lbs)

### Quality & Safety
- Maintain clean, organized job sites and properly store all tools and equipment
- Follow all safety protocols, wear required PPE, and work safely in all weather conditions
- Learn OSHA safety guidelines and best practices from experienced crew members
- Participate in daily crew meetings and safety briefings

### Learning & Growth
- Actively learn decorative concrete techniques, material science, and installation methods
- Ask questions, seek feedback, and build foundational trade skills
- Participate in informal training sessions with field leads and experienced installers
- Gain exposure to project management, scheduling, and client communication
