## Role Overview

As a Decorative Concrete Installer at 3 T's Engineering.AI, you'll be at the heart of our operations—learning, building, and mastering the craft of decorative concrete work. You'll work alongside experienced installers and field leads on residential and commercial projects throughout the St. Louis area. This is a **hands-on, outdoor-focused role** where you'll develop real expertise in concrete finishing, stamping, sealing, and resurfacing techniques.

This position is ideal for someone who:
- Enjoys working outdoors and isn't afraid of physical work
- Takes pride in craftsmanship and attention to detail
- Wants to **build a real career** in the skilled trades
- Is energetic, coachable, and ready to learn a valuable skill
- Values honest work and direct communication

**No prior concrete experience required**—we'll train you. What we need is your willingness to learn, your reliability, and your commitment to quality work.

## Key Responsibilities

### Daily Job Site Work
- Prepare concrete surfaces for resurfacing projects: grinding, etching, pressure washing, and removing debris
- Mix and apply concrete overlays, epoxies, sealers, and specialty coatings per project specifications
- Operate hand tools and power equipment: trowels, grinders, polishers, mixers, and finishing tools
- Apply decorative finishes including stamped patterns, color stains, and protective sealants
- Screed, float, and finish concrete surfaces to achieve smooth, uniform, high-quality results
- Consolidate concrete and remove voids using proper vibration and settling techniques
- Work on hands and knees, lift and carry materials (up to 100 lbs regularly), and perform physically demanding tasks

### Quality & Safety
- Follow all project specifications to ensure finished work meets client standards
- Maintain clean, organized job sites and properly store/care for all tools and equipment
- Follow all safety protocols, wear required PPE, and work safely in all weather conditions
- Attention to detail—catch issues early and deliver finishes that reflect our reputation
- Work effectively as part of a team with other installers, field leads, and crew members

### Learning & Growth
- Actively learn decorative concrete techniques, material science, and best practices
- Ask questions, seek feedback, and continuously improve your craft
- Take initiative in understanding project requirements and quality standards
- Build knowledge that positions you for advancement to Field Lead and beyond
