About 3T's Engineering.AI

Our team comprises highly credentialed engineers educated at top engineering universities, complemented by advanced business training through MBA programs. We bring decades of cumulative experience across the full spectrum of engineering disciplines and operational leadership.

Having held senior roles including International Engineer, Production Engineer, Packaging Engineer Manager, Project Engineering Manager, and Sustainability Core Team Manager, our team understands the challenges you face at every level of manufacturing and operations.

We work at the intersection of traditional engineering excellence and emerging technologies, with deep expertise in robotics, automation, and AI applications for industrial environments.

  • Capital Equipment Engineering & Selection

  • Robotics & Automation Integration
  • Process Optimization & Efficiency
  • Engineering & Operations Management
